Experimental results of the manuscript:

 

“Nonlocal sparse and low-rank regularization for optical flow estimation”

 

By Weisheng Dong, Guangming Shi, Xiaocheng Hu, and Yi Ma,

submitted to IEEE Trans. On Image Processing.

 

Note:

(1)   The optical flow estimation method using the spatial regularization is labeled as “SR”;

(2)   The optical flow estimation method using the nuclear-norm based low-rank regularization is labeled as “SR_LR_NN”;

(3)   The optical flow estimation method using the logdet function based low-rank regularization is labeled as “SR_LR_Logdet”;

(4)   The optical flow estimation method using the low-rank and sparse regularization is labeled as “SR_LRS_Logdet”;

(5)   The optical flow estimation method in [1] is denoted as Classic_NL.

Then, the flow estimated by the method SR_LRS_Logdet on test sequence Venus is labeled as “Venus_SR_LRS_Logdet”. Other result flow fields are labeled similarly. The ground truth is labeled as “Venus_gt”.
